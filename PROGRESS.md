- **03c_nonlinear_clustering.py** → evidence only, 03's k=15 KMeans is unchanged and untouched. Installed umap-learn/minisom/gensim/networkx. Findings: (1) hierarchical — average linkage wins cophenetic correlation (0.822) but its cuts at every k are **degenerate chaining** (488-456/492 notes in one mega-cluster, ARI vs KMeans@15 ~0.001-0.01); ward has much worse cophenetic correlation (0.273) but is the only linkage giving a balanced partition (largest cluster 34.6% at k=5) — caught and explicitly flagged rather than reporting the trivial "0.99 nesting purity" as a genuine finding, since it was really just "everything's in one blob." Saved `outputs/hierarchical_k5_superfamilies.csv` flagged degenerate. (2) UMAP(nn=15/30, min_dist=0)→HDBSCAN finds only 2 clusters either way, ARI~0.005 — confirms the earlier 03b HDBSCAN-finds-noise result even with the standard manifold-first pipeline. (3) skip-gram (gensim, sg=1, 50d) vs PPMI+SVD: ARI=0.32 — moderate agreement, expected per Levy & Goldberg 2014 (skip-gram implicitly factorises shifted PPMI). (4) 6x6 SOM: ARI=0.39 vs KMeans@15, u-matrix has no sharp ridges (range 0.31-1.0, patchy not bimodal) — continuity, not discrete islands. (5) Louvain on PPMI≥1.0 graph (12,990 edges): 16 communities, modularity 0.25 (below the 0.3 weak-structure line), ARI=0.22. All five methods independently point the same direction: real structure but no natural discrete boundaries at any k — consistent with 03b's conclusion that k=15 is a chosen discretisation, not a discovered one.
