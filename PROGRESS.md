# Progress log

One line per finished script: what was produced, row counts, anything surprising.

- **01_clean.py** → `data/interim/01_products.parquet` (131,930×30), `01_notes_long.parquet` (1,098,223 rows), `01_accords_long.parquet` (1,000,570 rows), `01_ai_summary_long.parquet` (210,521 rows). `in_population` (people≥50, ≥1 note, year 1990-2025) = 32,499 products. Surprising: the schema's "vote aggregates go null together" claim doesn't hold in this dump — `people` goes null independently of relation/seasons/daypart/community_gender, which stay present as real all-zero dicts; added `has_votes` to null those groups out only when their own dict sums to zero, checked per-group, not inferred from `people`.
- **02_normalise_notes.py** → `outputs/note_normalisation.csv` (2,029 raw tokens → 492 canonical notes after review). Surprising: naive word-containment auto-merge is dangerous — it transitively chained "sandalwood" into a 450-token/30,782-product blob (apple, rose, tea, chocolate...) via hub words like "water"/"oil"; rebuilt around edit-distance-gated fuzzy matching + a "remainder isn't itself a real word" guard, plus a hardcoded manual override list (ambergris/amberwood un-merged from amber, clover/straw/mango un-merged, virginia cedar→cedar and mandarin orange→mandarin promoted) for full reproducibility.
- **03_taxonomy.py** → `outputs/taxonomy_map.csv` (492 notes × 15 clusters), `outputs/taxonomy_validation.csv` (top-5 accords by lift/cluster, accords with population count <100 excluded as unstable), `data/interim/03_product_family.parquet` (103,492 rows, fractional membership). Silhouette: k=12→0.037, k=15→0.031 (used), k=18→0.044. Surprising: cluster 4 ("soapy") is a real, tightly-separated ~76-note cluster in SVD space but it's essentially the "ubiquitous backbone notes" bucket (musk, bergamot, sandalwood, vanilla, jasmine, amber, patchouli, cedar, rose...) — it holds ~59% of all launches, which dominates cluster 4's supply share downstream. Worth a look before renaming clusters.
- **04_demand.py** → `data/interim/04_family_demand.parquet` (15 families × raw/demeaned approval, desire, adoption, value). 0 nulls on all four signals within population (people≥50 guarantees relation/rating always present). 93.4% of products got brand-demeaned (1,160/2,261 population brands have ≥5 products).
- **05_sentiment.py** → `data/interim/05_family_sentiment.parquet`. Subpopulation (in_population AND has_ai_summary) = 11,579 products (35.6% of population), skews toward more-voted/slightly-newer/slightly-higher-rated products (mean people 1,332 vs 577; mean year 2016.9 vs 2015.8; mean rating 4.00 vs 3.96) — expected, ai_summary is concentrated on popular products.
- **05b_seasonality.py** → `data/interim/05b_family_season.parquet`. All 32,499 population products had season votes (has_votes always true in-population). Oud/whiskey families skew heavily winter/autumn; terpenic (coconut/salty/tropical) skews summer — directionally sane.
- **06_whitespace.py** → `outputs/06_territory_scores.csv` (15 families, every component column visible), `data/interim/06_family_share_by_year.parquet`. Used brand-demeaned approval/desire in the composite score (a modeling choice, not a CLAUDE.md fixed decision — flagged for review). Top 3 by score: cluster 11 (savory→gourmand-nutty), 7 (oud), 2 (balsamic). Cluster 4 (soapy/ubiquitous) is bottom by a wide margin, driven by its outsized supply share.
- **07_figures.py** → `outputs/figures/{a_note_map,b_launch_share_over_time,c_demand_vs_supply}.png`, 200dpi.
